Hi, I am **Huiyu Yang**, a PhD researcher at the Center for Machine Vision and Signal Analysis (CMVS), University of Oulu, Finland.

My research focuses on **multimodal learning, affective computing, and physiological signal analysis**, with a particular interest in **video-based remote photoplethysmography (rPPG)**.

I work on:
- Video-based rPPG reconstruction and morphology modeling
- Multimodal learning (Video / Audio / Text)
- Affective computing and emotion recognition
